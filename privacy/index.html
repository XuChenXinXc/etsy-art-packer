Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At EtsyArtPacker ("we", "our", or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your intellectual property. This Privacy Policy explains how our tool operates and our zero-data-collection philosophy.

1. Zero Server Upload Architecture

EtsyArtPacker operates entirely client-side within your web browser using HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript APIs. When you drag and drop artwork into our resizer:

  • Your original and resized files never leave your local device.
  • No images, metadata, or creative assets are uploaded, transmitted, stored, or processed on our servers.
  • All ratio cropping, 300 DPI metadata injection, and ZIP packaging occur locally in your browser memory.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect or store your uploaded image files.
  • We do not require account registration or store credit card numbers on our servers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade any personal data or creative assets to third parties.

3. Analytics & Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to serve non-intrusive advertisements that help keep EtsyArtPacker 100% free and open-source. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. We also use lightweight aggregated analytics (Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights) to measure page views and site performance without tracking personally identifiable information.

4. Third-Party Services & Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Your artwork is processed only in your browser and is never sent to us. However, this website loads a few third-party services that may receive limited technical data, such as your IP address, browser and device information, and cookie identifiers:

  • Google AdSense: advertising cookies, as described above.
  • Google Fonts: font files are loaded from Google's servers.
  • Vercel Web Analytics & Speed Insights: aggregated page views and performance metrics.

These providers process such data under their own privacy policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can opt out of personalized ads through Google's Ads Settings and block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access or delete your personal data. We do not run user accounts or store personal data ourselves, so requests about advertising data should be directed to Google; for any other privacy request, contact us below.
