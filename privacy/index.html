Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

EtsyArtPacker is a free, open-source browser tool. We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data or images. All image processing, cropping, resizing, 300 DPI metadata injection, and ZIP packaging happen locally in your web browser. No files are uploaded to our servers or any third-party cloud.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No account registration: You never need to sign up, sign in, or provide an email to use the service.
  • No image uploads: Your artwork files never leave your device. All Canvas processing executes entirely inside your client browser environment.
  • No payment details: The core tool is 100% free forever; we do not collect credit card, billing, or financial data.
  • No invasive tracking: We do not use third-party advertising trackers or data brokers. Only minimal, anonymous, privacy-friendly aggregated performance metrics (such as page load time via Vercel Speed Insights) are utilized.

2. Third Parties & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data with advertisers, data brokers, or third-party marketplaces. Because no images or personal identities are transmitted to our servers, we have no customer data to share.

3. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we do not store, process, or retain personal data on our servers, data access, portability, and right-to-be-forgotten requests do not apply to your artworks. If you contact our support desk via email, we will only use your contact information to respond directly to your inquiry and will permanently delete it upon request.

4. Support & Contact

If you have questions about privacy, open-source compliance, or technical details, feel free to reach out to our dedicated support channel:

support@etsy-art-packer.vercel.app

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
